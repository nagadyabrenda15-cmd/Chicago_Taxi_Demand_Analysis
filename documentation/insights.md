# Key Insights

## Overview

The analysis identified clear patterns in Chicago taxi demand, payment behaviour, location activity and company performance.

The findings below are based on the cleaned taxi trip records analysed in Power BI.

## 1. Demand Changes Throughout the Day

Taxi activity changes significantly depending on the time of day.

The analysis identified **17:00 as the busiest hour**, with **21,245 trips**.

The periods **06:00–09:00** and **16:00–19:00** were important demand periods, with late-day activity being particularly notable.

### Business Meaning

Taxi operators can use these patterns to plan driver and vehicle availability around periods of higher demand.

---

## 2. Thursday Recorded the Highest Daily Trip Volume

Thursday recorded the highest number of trips with:

**51,606 trips**

Wednesday followed with:

**50,884 trips**

### Business Meaning

Higher-demand days can be considered when planning driver availability and operational resources.

---

## 3. Near North Side Was a Major Location Hub

**Near North Side** ranked first for both trip starts and trip endings.

This indicates strong two-way taxi activity in the area.

### Business Meaning

The area could be important for:

- Vehicle staging
- Driver positioning
- Curb-access planning
- Managing pickup and drop-off activity

---

## 4. Credit Card Was the Largest Recorded Payment Type

Credit Card was the largest recorded payment category in the analysis.

Card users tipped approximately:

**22.97% of fares**

Tipping behaviour also varied across fare levels and payment types.

### Business Meaning

Payment behaviour can provide useful information for understanding customer behaviour and revenue patterns.

A limitation is that cash tips may not always be recorded in the same way as card tips.

---

## 5. Flash Cab Had the Highest Trip Volume

Flash Cab recorded the highest trip volume among the companies analysed.

It represented approximately:

**22.16% market share**

Flash Cab also showed:

- Approximately 59K trips
- Average fare of $22.08
- Card tip rate of 22.01%
- Revenue per mile of $3.49

### Business Meaning

Flash Cab provides a useful high-volume benchmark for comparing taxi company performance.

However, smaller companies may show extreme values because their trip volumes are lower.

---

## 6. Overall Business View

The dashboard displayed approximately:

- **268K trips**
- **$6.09M revenue**
- **5.83 miles average trip distance**
- **22.97% card tip percentage**

These measures provide a high-level view of taxi activity during the analysis period.

---

# Key Business Recommendations

## Recommendation 1 — Plan for Late-Day Demand

Pilot additional driver or vehicle availability between:

**16:00–19:00**

Thursday should receive particular attention because it recorded the highest daily trip volume.

A four-week pilot could be used to evaluate whether additional availability improves service during the busiest period.

---

## Recommendation 2 — Improve Near North Side Operations

Develop a staging and curb-access plan for the Near North Side.

Pickup and drop-off activity should be monitored by shift to identify suitable areas for vehicle positioning.

---

## Recommendation 3 — Use Company Performance Benchmarks

Use Flash Cab's high trip volume as a benchmark when comparing company performance.

A monthly scorecard could compare:

- Trips
- Market share
- Average fare
- Card tipping
- Revenue per mile

---

## Important Interpretation Note

The findings describe patterns in the available taxi records.

For example, lower average speed during a busy period may suggest operational delays, but **volume and speed alone do not prove that traffic congestion caused the lower speed**.

Further operational or traffic data would be required to establish causation.
